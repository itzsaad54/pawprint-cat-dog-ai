Under the hood

02 / Numbers from training
98.5%Test accuracy
224²Input pixels
2.2MParameters
1,200Images used

Transfer learning on MobileNetV2 pre-trained on ImageNet. The base was frozen for the first stage, then the last 30 layers were fine-tuned at a learning rate of 1e-5. Output is a single sigmoid neuron: below 0.5 is a cat, above is a dog.
